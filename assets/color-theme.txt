# **"Story Tree" Wedding Website Theme Ideas**

## **Core Theme Concept: "The Family Tree of Love"**

### **Primary Color Palette:**
- **Deep Bark Brown**: #3C2A1E (Primary background/navigation)
- **Forest Green**: #2E4F2B (Accent color, buttons)
- **Blossom Pink**: #F4C2C2 (Wedding/romantic accent)
- **Cream/Off-White**: #F8F4E6 (Background/typography)
- **Gold Leaf**: #D4AF37 (Highlights, metallic elements)
- **Soil Brown**: #654321 (Secondary accents)

### **Visual Motifs:**

#### **1. Tree & Branch Elements:**
- **Navigation Menu** as extending branches
- **Logo** incorporating intertwined tree & wedding rings
- **Section dividers** as vine or root patterns
- **Family trees** showing how two families merge

#### **2. Seasonal Wedding Imagery:**
- **Spring**: Cherry blossoms, new leaves (engagement/pre-wedding)
- **Summer**: Full, lush greenery (wedding season)
- **Autumn**: Golden leaves, falling (harvest/mature love)
- **Winter**: Bare branches with fairy lights (anniversary/winter weddings)

#### **3. Symbolic Elements:**
- **Roots** for "Our Foundation" or "How We Met"
- **Branches** for "Our Journey Together"
- **Leaves** representing family members/guests
- **Fruit/Blossoms** for "Our Future" or "Children"
- **Birds/Nests** for "Home" or "New Beginnings"

### **Page-by-Page Theme Ideas:**

#### **Homepage:** 
"The Wedding Tree" - A growing tree with photos hanging like fruit

#### **Our Story Page:** 
"Two Roots, One Tree" - Showing two separate root systems growing into one tree

#### **Gallery Page:** 
"Leaves of Memory" - Photos arranged like leaves on branches

#### **Wedding Details:**
"Branching Out" - Information spreading like tree branches

#### **RSVP/Guestbook:**
"Add Your Leaf" - Guests add messages to digital leaves on the tree

#### **Registry:**
"Helping Our Tree Grow" - Gifts visualized as watering cans, sunlight, etc.

### **Typography Style:**
- **Headings**: Rustic, hand-drawn or calligraphy fonts
- **Body Text**: Clean, readable serif fonts
- **Script Elements**: Romantic cursive for quotes

### **Animation/Motion Ideas:**
- Gentle leaf fall in background
- Branches growing as you scroll
- Blossoms blooming on hover
- Roots spreading at the footer

### **Seasonal Variations (Optional):**
Offer couples to choose their wedding season theme:
- **Spring Romance**: Pastel pinks, soft greens, cherry blossoms
- **Summer Love**: Vibrant greens, sunshine golds, full foliage
- **Autumn Harvest**: Burgundy, burnt orange, golden yellows
- **Winter Wonderland**: Evergreens, silver accents, bare elegant branches

### **Special Touches:**
- **Family Tree Integration**: Visualize how two families merge
- **Timeline as Growth Rings**: Show relationship milestones as tree rings
- **Love Notes on Leaves**: Share messages on digital leaves
- **Interactive Tree Planting**: Plant a virtual tree for the couple
