Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our packages and services

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely! We understand that every wedding is unique. We offer customizable packages where you can add or remove services based on your specific needs and budget. Contact us for a personalized quote.

What's included in the "Edited Photos"?

All packages include professionally edited high-resolution photos. Our editing process includes color correction, exposure adjustments, cropping, and basic retouching. You'll receive a mix of color and black & white images.
