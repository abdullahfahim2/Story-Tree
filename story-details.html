RAINA & ANKUSH

Their Story

The first time they met, it almost didn’t happen at all. What began as a reluctant coffee turned into hours of conversation that neither wanted to end.

There was no grand declaration, no dramatic moment — just a quiet understanding. Over time, shared laughter, long-distance calls, and unexpected surprises shaped a love that felt effortless and inevitable.

Their wedding was not just a celebration — it was a reflection of everything they are: intimate, emotional, and beautifully unscripted.
