FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Get answers to common questions about our wedding photography and cinematography services

1

Where is The Wedding Story based? Do you travel worldwide?

We are based in Mumbai (India) and Boston (USA), and we travel all across the world to shoot weddings. Some of our best weddings have been shot in locations like:

Istanbul Puerto Rico Barcelona Dubai Montreux Seychelles Marrakech and many others

Wherever your love story takes you, we'll be there to capture every moment. Our team is passionate about documenting weddings in diverse cultural settings and breathtaking locations around the globe.

2

What is the typical team strength of TWS team that will be traveling to cover my wedding? Will a reduction in crew affect the cost?

We usually film with a comprehensive team that includes:

  • 3-4 cinematographers (candid style)
  • 2-3 photographers (candid and documentary style)
  • A director
  • A production manager
  • An aerial drone operator

The crew works as one coherent team with different cameramen specializing in different lenses and styles including aerial, time-lapse and underwater filming.

For Larger Scale Weddings:

We don't recommend a reduction in the size of the crew as this is likely to affect the quality and grandeur of the film. This choreographed, multi-camera setup allows us to position ourselves strategically to capture ceremonies more naturally as they take place, without any hindrance.

For Intimate Weddings:

There are certain advantages to having a smaller team too! For more intimate weddings, a crew size of 4-5 people is enough. This allows for greater, non-obtrusive individual control of filming for each crew member and this can lead to better, unobstructed views of the festivities especially when the party is on a smaller scale involving lesser people.

This also creates a warmer bond between the crew and families as there are fewer people to co-ordinate with. There is an obvious reduction in cost too!
